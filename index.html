NEXT = how often price closed that way after the same RSI zone in past data. It is history, not a prediction. noise = same as a coin flip. few = too little data. check = worth re-testing on other data, not a signal. With many pairs, about 1 in 20 looks special by pure luck. L3 = last 3 candles (U up, D down).

Rule: RSI crosses below the low level = UP, above the high level = DOWN, one trade at a time. Break-even is the win rate needed at your payout. Yahoo forex data is not identical to the broker feed, so results are only an approximation.
